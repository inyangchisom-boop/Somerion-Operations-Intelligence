Privacy Policy

How the current Somerion website and workspace handle information.

Effective date: 28 November 2026 · Version 1.0

1. About this policy

This policy describes the information handled by the current Somerion Operations Intelligence website and its early-access workspace. Somerion is an early-stage project. This page describes the implementation reviewed for this release; features and service providers may change as the product develops.

This is a plain-language product disclosure, not a statement that Somerion has completed a legal compliance audit. The website has not been independently audited for privacy or security compliance.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Account information: email address and authentication-related account identifiers used for registration, sign-in, email verification and password reset.
  • Workspace records: information you choose to enter, such as order references, customer names and contact details, order totals and currency, product names, SKUs, quantities, reorder levels, and other business records supported by the workspace.
  • Feedback: rating, category and written feedback you submit. The interface states that feedback is private unless Somerion approves it for publication.
  • Integration information: connection status and, where a provider connection is implemented and you choose to configure it, provider credentials or authorization data. The current app includes PayPal Sandbox and WooCommerce connection flows; availability and live-mode restrictions depend on backend configuration.
  • Operational activity: certain integration actions may send event names and related details to Somerion's integration service for operational records.
  • Technical information: information that Firebase, Cloudflare, your browser, or other infrastructure providers may process to provide, secure and operate their services. The exact logs and retention periods are controlled in part by those providers and current configuration.

3. Why information is used

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Save and display workspace records belonging to your account.
  • Provide, verify and troubleshoot integration features you choose to use.
  • Receive and review product feedback.
  • Protect the service, investigate errors and maintain operational reliability.
  • Respond to messages and requests sent to the contact address below.

4. Where information is processed

The reviewed website uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for account authentication and workspace records. It also communicates with Somerion's Cloudflare-hosted integration service for integration-related functions and operational events. When you choose to use an external provider connection, that provider may separately process information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Google Fonts is loaded from Google's font service by the website. Your browser may make a request to that service when a page uses the hosted fonts. Somerion does not control the provider's independent processing.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Somerion does not sell personal information. Information is made available to infrastructure providers as needed to operate the service, and to an external platform only when a feature you choose requires that exchange. Do not connect an account or submit information unless you are authorized to do so. The current product is not a general-purpose public directory of your private workspace records.

6. Security and access

The current Firestore rules are designed to restrict supported workspace records to the authenticated account that owns them. Some integration credentials are intended to be handled by the backend rather than browser code. These design measures do not guarantee that a system is free from vulnerabilities. The product remains under development and has not completed an independent security audit. Do not use it as the sole repository for highly sensitive, regulated, payment-card, health, or government-identification information.

7. Retention, access and deletion requests

The current project does not publish a complete, verified data-retention schedule or provide a fully automated in-app account and data deletion workflow. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information, email somerion.operations@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and describe the request. We will review what can be actioned and may need to verify account ownership. Some records may remain in backups, security logs or provider systems for periods determined by their configurations and policies.

8. Children

The workspace is intended for people using it for business or professional operations, not for children. Do not create an account for a child or submit a child's personal information.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the product changes. The effective date and version above identify this published version. Questions or privacy requests: somerion.operations@gmail.com.
